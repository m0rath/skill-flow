WHAT JUSTOOLS IS

AI instructions are hard to see. We make them visible.

A skill file, a Markdown playbook or even a whole codebase is just text, until something goes wrong. Our tools turn that text into something you can look at, challenge and improve.

>

Map

See the workflow of any skill, playbook or codebase as a diagram or mind map — with what it creates, what it needs and the rules that apply.

?

Question

Let AI find where the instructions are vague, missing a step or likely to break. Chat with it, or read its suggestions on your own.

✓

Fix

Turn a suggestion into an edit, review it in the Changes tab and download the cleaned-up files when you are happy.

HOW HUMANS MAKE AI BETTER

A loop, not a handoff.

AI is fast at reading and flagging. People know what the work is actually for. Each round of review makes the instructions clearer, and clearer instructions give more reliable AI results.

01

You bring the file or project

A SKILL.md, a Markdown playbook, a project folder, a .zip or a public GitHub repo.

02

AI maps and questions it

It draws the workflow or architecture, then flags gaps, vague rules and missing inputs.

03

You review every change

Suggestions arrive as proposals. Accept, edit or dismiss each one.

04

The next run is better

Clearer instructions give more reliable AI output, and you can export the result.

THE AI

Does the heavy reading

  • Draws the workflow as a diagram or mind map
  • Spots gaps, vague rules and missing inputs
  • Proposes edits and explains why
YOU

Keep the final say

  • Pick the AI: Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, with your own API key
  • Accept, edit or dismiss every suggestion, or allow automatic fixes
  • Own the final file and export it as .md, PNG, SVG or Mermaid
